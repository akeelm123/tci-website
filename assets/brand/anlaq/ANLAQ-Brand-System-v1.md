## Positioning
ANLAQ is an independent advisory firm for boards and senior leaders responsible for consequential technology, AI and transformation decisions. It connects evidence, judgement and business value through the Transformation Confidence Index™.

## Character
Clear. Considered. Independent. Executive.

Primary archetype: **Sage**.  
Secondary influence: **Ruler**, used only to add restraint and authority.

## Logo
The supplied approved artwork is the source of truth.

Production rules:
- Preserve the ANLAQ letterforms and the distinctive two-tone Q.
- Do not redraw, substitute or typeset the wordmark.
- Do not recolour the Q outside approved variants.
- No gradients in production logo artwork.
- Maintain clear space of at least the cap-height of the `A`.
- Minimum recommended width: 120 px for the primary lock-up; use the Q mark below this where legibility requires.
- Use the reversed or monochrome version when the primary mark lacks sufficient contrast.

## Graphic language
Use evidence markers, confidence ranges, decision pathways, timelines, restrained lines and TCI™ relationships. Avoid generic AI imagery, glowing brains, robots, circuit-board heads, stock handshakes and decorative technology clichés.

## Voice
Write in British English. ANLAQ is clear, considered, independent and executive. Prefer evidence and specific statements over consultancy slogans. Avoid inflated claims and generic AI language.

## Digital system
Spacing: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96 / 128 px  
Radius: 4 / 8 / 12 px  
Use shadows sparingly. Create hierarchy mainly with typography, whitespace, contrast and rules.
