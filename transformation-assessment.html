How it works

I start with the original investment thesis and test it against what has actually happened: investment, delivery, adoption, technology and data capability, organisational change, sustainable capability, risk and value realised.

The assessment covers all seven TCI dimensions. Material claims are tested against evidence, and executive interviews are used to examine assumptions, accountability, ownership and contradictions.

Evidence and engagement

Relevant evidence may include the original business case and investment papers, financials and expenditure, delivery measures, technology and architecture evidence, data evidence, risks and issues, adoption and organisational-change evidence, benefits and value-realisation evidence, governance and decision records and other relevant operating evidence.

Material claims are checked against the available record rather than accepted from questionnaire responses.

What makes it different

The flagship ANLAQ assessment. It connects the original investment case to what has actually been delivered, the conditions for sustainable change and the value realised.

It provides the deepest evidence review and broadest independent TCI judgement across the four services.

What you receive

A Transformation Confidence Executive Report and TCI Confidence Profile, including independent qualitative confidence judgements, evidence findings, material confidence gaps, value implications, priority actions and executive decision considerations.
