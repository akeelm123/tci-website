Illustrative scenario 5

Transformation reporting green, value confidence red

Budget on track. Milestones delivered. Risks managed. Dependencies tracked. The programme appeared well controlled. But the executive sponsor remained uneasy.

This is a fictional teaching scenario, not a client engagement. Evidence gaps and timelines illustrate discussion only; they are not measured client results or a recommended client plan.

Executive concern

What evidence shows this transformation is creating value?

Business units were not reporting visible performance improvement. Operational teams felt burdened by change. Benefits remained theoretical. The Board asked the direct question.

Illustrative decision evidence

Evidence to weigh

Fictional example for discussion. These are questions to test, not measured TCI™ results.

What appears supported
Budget, milestones, risks and dependencies are being reported.
What remains uncertain
Business performance and realised benefits have not yet matched the green programme status.
Evidence to test next
Ask benefit owners for baseline measures, observed business results and the conditions required for sustained value.

Executive interpretation

The programme is well managed but not yet value-proven.

Delivery confidence is higher than transformation confidence. This is one of the most common failure patterns in enterprise transformation: the organisation is successfully delivering work but cannot demonstrate that the business is becoming stronger.

Example actions for discussion

30days

Stabilise

Revalidate the original benefits case. Assign named business owners for each major benefit. Identify benefits already realised, at risk or unsupported by evidence. Stop reporting benefits separately from delivery.

90days

Strengthen

Redefine programme reporting around outcomes, not only milestones. Connect delivery releases to measurable business indicators. Review whether scope should change to protect value. Establish Board-level value assurance.

180days

Sustain

Track benefits after implementation. Embed value realisation into business-as-usual ownership. Use TCI™ quarterly to assess whether confidence is improving.

"Green delivery does not guarantee transformation success. A programme can be on track and still fail to create meaningful value."
