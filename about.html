About Akeel Advisory.

Akeel Advisory is an independent Technology • AI • Transformation firm working across industries. Founder Akeel Munshi brings 30 years of technology experience, including global leadership roles at Barclays and Standard Chartered and earlier consulting at CGI.

Akeel Munshi, founder

What the investment has delivered, and what it has not.

Akeel Munshi has led technology, data, engineering and enterprise transformation across complex, regulated organisations.

His experience spans global platforms, Agile and DevOps, innovation, operational excellence, data strategy and large-scale organisational change. This work has placed him at the point where strategic intent meets delivery pressure, investment scrutiny and enterprise risk.

He created the Transformation Confidence Index™ to help leadership teams test whether their transformation narrative is supported by credible, connected evidence.
