Experience

Strategy, architecture and the work of delivery.

Most recently, I led the strategy and evolution of Standard Chartered’s Global Data Platform. My work covered platform architecture, data-product capabilities and the operating model needed to support enterprise data, AI and advanced analytics.

Engineering and organisational change
