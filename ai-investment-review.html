Best suited for

How it works

I apply all seven TCI dimensions through an AI-specific lens, examining the investment case, strategic fit, use cases, adoption, technology and data readiness, operating economics, controls, risk and realised value.

Targeted interviews with business, AI, technology, finance, data and risk stakeholders test the investment thesis, benefit claims and assumptions behind further scale.

Evidence and engagement

Relevant evidence may include AI business cases and investment papers, use-case portfolios, expenditure and forecasts, benefit assumptions, adoption and usage data, model and platform architecture, data and retrieval controls, MLOps/LLMOps evidence, operating costs, risk and exception registers, governance decisions and production performance.

Material evidence is classified as documented, corroborated, asserted or unknown. Evidence quality remains separate from the confidence judgement.

What makes it different

A specialist application of TCI designed specifically for AI investment and scale decisions. It connects technical readiness with adoption, operating economics, risk and evidence of business value.

It is not simply a smaller Transformation Confidence Assessment. Its focus is the specific executive question of whether the evidence supports further AI investment or scale.

What you receive

An AI Investment Confidence Report setting out the independent executive judgement, TCI confidence profile, evidence findings, value position, material confidence gaps, scale considerations, priority actions and executive decision considerations.

Decision supported

Designed to help executives determine whether the evidence supports further investment, scale, conditional progression, corrective action or reconsideration of an AI investment.
