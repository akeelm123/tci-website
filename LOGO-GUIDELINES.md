# Approved ANLAQ serif logo

The approved identity combines the open framing symbol with the serif ANLAQ wordmark and champagne Q tail. Use the supplied artwork without redrawing or re-typesetting it.

## Source of truth
`assets/brand/anlaq-vector-v1/` contains the approved outlined SVGs, font licences, PNG social image and browser icons. These files are restored byte-for-byte from the approved September rollout.

Use `anlaq-primary-no-descriptor.svg` for light headers and `anlaq-reversed-no-descriptor.svg` for dark footers. Use the full primary artwork at larger sizes and in organisation metadata. Preserve its aspect ratio and clear space. Use the supplied favicon for small browser placements.

Navy: #0F2744. Champagne: #C6A15B. Ivory: #FBF8F3.

The superseded October sans-serif refinement and its generated review files were removed from the current repository. They remain recoverable in Git history. Other historical artwork is retained and must not replace the approved SVGs.
