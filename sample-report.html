Illustrative executive report extract

Should an AI pilot receive production funding?

Fictional scenario · Proposed report format
This example illustrates how a review could present evidence and decision considerations. It is not client work, a completed assessment or a recommendation about a real investment.

A pilot has shown technical promise. Leadership needs to decide whether the available evidence supports a wider production commitment.

Discuss your AI investment decision ↗

Executive consideration

Consider staged funding while the operating costs, adoption assumptions and ownership of benefits are tested. Successful pilot delivery alone would not establish the case for wider rollout.

In this scenario, management would need to define the conditions for each further commitment and retain accountability for the decision.

Separate the evidence from the claim

Evidence stateIllustrative finding
DocumentedPilot usage records and a technical evaluation are available in this fictional scenario.
CorroboratedNo independently corroborated productivity gain is assumed in this example.
AssertedThe investment proposal anticipates productivity gains across the wider organisation.
UnknownFull operating costs, sustained adoption and benefit ownership have not yet been established.

Questions across the seven TCI™ dimensions

These are illustrative lines of enquiry, not scored findings or a formal confidence profile.

DimensionEvidence to examine
Strategic AlignmentThe intended productivity outcome is stated. Its contribution to the wider business case needs testing.
Leadership CommitmentA pilot sponsor is identified. Ownership of benefits after expansion needs confirmation.
Technology ReadinessA technical evaluation exists. Production resilience and support evidence need review.
People & CulturePilot usage records are available. Broader adoption and changes to working practices remain untested.
Data & AI ConfidenceData access, output reliability and exception handling need examination for the proposed production setting.
Delivery ExcellenceExpansion milestones need to be linked to measurable readiness conditions.
Business Value RealisationProductivity gains are asserted. Baselines, operating costs and attributable benefits need corroboration.

Before the next commitment

  1. Finance and the business sponsor: review the full operating-cost assumptions and identify accountable benefit owners.
  2. Business teams: establish a baseline and test whether measured usage produces the expected change in work.
  3. Technology, data and risk owners: review production reliability, access controls and exception handling.
  4. The decision owner: agree evidence-based conditions for releasing the next funding tranche.

What an actual engagement would add

A commissioned review would examine the agreed evidence, test material claims with stakeholders and set out the resulting judgement, limitations and decision considerations. Its scope and outputs would be agreed before work starts.
