The offerings

Four distinct levels of evidence and judgement.

Each offering can stand on its own. The services are not a mandatory sequence. Select the review that best fits the decision you face.

Initial · free · self-ledTCI™ Self-AssessmentA free, self-led starting point to identify where harder questions may need to be asked.

Where should we be asking harder questions?

Best suited for

Leaders seeking an initial view before deciding whether independent investigation is warranted.

How it works

Twenty-one structured questions across all seven TCI™ dimensions invite leaders to reflect on the conditions for sustainable value. Responses capture the participant’s current view. No supporting evidence is required.

Evidence and engagement

The assessment is completed by the participant. There is no independent evidence review and no stakeholder interview process.

What makes it different

It provides a fast, accessible starting point for leaders who want to identify areas of strength, uncertainty or potential concern before deciding whether deeper investigation is required.

What you receive

An indicative TCI™ confidence profile highlighting strengths, uncertainty and areas that may warrant further investigation.

What it does not do

This is not an independent assessment, audit, assurance opinion or evidence-based investment recommendation.

The existing introductory 21-question discussion guide is a separate five-theme resource.

Focused · paid · independentTCI™ Executive DiagnosticA focused first review when something feels wrong but the cause is unclear.

Where is confidence weakest, and what should we investigate?

Best suited for

Transformations with a material concern that needs locating before a deeper assessment is commissioned.

How it works

We examine selected evidence across the seven TCI™ dimensions at a high level and speak with a small number of senior stakeholders. The scope stays deliberately narrow.

Evidence and engagement

Selected material evidence is reviewed, supported by a limited number of senior stakeholder interviews. The purpose is to identify where confidence appears weakest rather than conduct a comprehensive assessment.

What makes it different

A rapid independent diagnosis without committing to the scope and depth of a full assessment.

It is deliberately a focused engagement, not a reduced version of the Transformation Confidence Assessment™.

What you receive

A concise Executive Confidence Diagnostic identifying the main confidence gaps, supporting evidence and areas for further investigation.

Decision supported

Whether a deeper review is justified and where it should focus.

Specialist · paid · AI-specificAI Investment Confidence Review™Know whether your AI investment has the evidence to justify further funding or scale.

Does the evidence justify further AI investment or scale?

Best suited for

Organisations moving from AI experimentation to production, scaling GenAI programmes, reviewing an AI portfolio, or reconsidering further investment in an existing AI initiative or portfolio.

How it works

We apply all seven TCI™ dimensions through an AI-specific lens, examining the investment case, strategic fit, use cases, adoption, technology and data readiness, operating economics, controls, risk and realised value.

Targeted interviews with business, AI, technology, finance, data and risk stakeholders test the investment thesis, benefit claims and assumptions behind further scale.

Evidence and engagement

Relevant evidence may include AI business cases and investment papers, use-case portfolios, expenditure and forecasts, benefit assumptions, adoption and usage data, model and platform architecture, data and retrieval controls, MLOps/LLMOps evidence, operating costs, risk and exception registers, governance decisions and production performance.

Material evidence is classified as documented, corroborated, asserted or unknown. Evidence quality remains separate from the confidence judgement.

What makes it different

A specialist application of TCI™ designed specifically for AI investment and scale decisions. It connects technical readiness with adoption, operating economics, risk and evidence of business value.

It is not simply a smaller Transformation Confidence Assessment™. Its focus is the specific executive question of whether the evidence supports further AI investment or scale.

What you receive

An AI Investment Confidence Report™ setting out the independent executive judgement, TCI™ confidence profile, evidence findings, value position, material confidence gaps, scale considerations, priority actions and executive decision considerations.

Decision supported

Designed to help executives determine whether the evidence supports further investment, scale, conditional progression, corrective action or reconsideration of an AI investment.

Comprehensive · flagship assessmentTransformation Confidence Assessment™The comprehensive independent assessment of whether a major transformation is creating the value it was funded to deliver.

Are we creating the value we funded, and what does the evidence support next?

Best suited for

Executives facing a significant investment, intervention or transformation decision across technology, data, cloud, ERP, digital, platform, AI or operating-model change.

How it works

We start with the original investment thesis and test it against what has actually happened: investment, delivery, adoption, technology and data capability, organisational change, sustainable capability, risk and value realised.

The assessment covers all seven TCI™ dimensions. Material claims are tested against evidence, and executive interviews are used to examine assumptions, accountability, ownership and contradictions.

Evidence and engagement

Relevant evidence may include the original business case and investment papers, financials and expenditure, delivery measures, technology and architecture evidence, data evidence, risks and issues, adoption and organisational-change evidence, benefits and value-realisation evidence, governance and decision records and other relevant operating evidence.

Material claims are checked against the available record rather than accepted from questionnaire responses.

What makes it different

The flagship Akeel Advisory assessment. It connects the original investment case to what has actually been delivered, the conditions for sustainable change and the value realised.

It provides the deepest evidence review and broadest independent TCI™ judgement across the four services.

What you receive

A Transformation Confidence Executive Report™ and TCI™ Confidence Profile, including independent qualitative confidence judgements, evidence findings, material confidence gaps, value implications, priority actions and executive decision considerations.

Decision supported

Designed to inform a consequential executive investment, intervention or transformation decision.

Evidence before opinion

TCI™ separates what is known from what is believed.

Material evidence is classified as documented, corroborated, asserted or unknown. Evidence quality remains separate from the confidence judgement.

This allows executives to see not only what management believes, but how strongly the available evidence supports it.

Akeel Advisory does not turn questionnaire responses into an automatic transformation score. Evidence, management assertions and independent judgement remain distinct.

DocumentedA record exists.
CorroboratedIndependent sources agree.
AssertedA claim awaits support.
UnknownThe evidence is absent or unclear.

Powered by TCI™

One framework. Different depths of review.

The same seven TCI™ dimensions provide the underlying framework across the services. What changes is the depth of evidence, degree of independent challenge, scope of judgement and importance of the decision being supported. TCI™ confidence judgements remain qualitative and do not rely on an unvalidated numerical maturity score.

Independent by design

The boundary protects the judgement.

Akeel Advisory does not sell the technology being assessed or depend on implementation work resulting from its findings.

Our role is to examine the evidence, challenge assumptions and provide an independent view of the decision.

Our fee does not depend on whether the evidence supports further investment, corrective action, reset or reconsideration. Akeel Advisory informs the decision; management and the Board retain accountability for making it.
