TCI™ Self-AssessmentA free starting point to see where transformation confidence may need closer examination.

Where should we be asking harder questions?

How it works

Twenty-one structured questions across all seven TCI™ dimensions invite leaders to reflect on the conditions for sustainable value. Responses capture the participant’s current view; no supporting evidence is required.

Best suited for

Leaders seeking an initial view before deciding whether independent investigation is warranted.

What you receive

An indicative profile highlighting strengths, uncertainty and areas to investigate.

What it does not do

This is not an independent assessment, audit, assurance opinion or evidence-based investment recommendation. The existing introductory 21-question discussion guide is a separate five-theme resource.

TCI™ Executive DiagnosticA focused first review when something feels wrong but the cause is unclear.

Where is confidence weakest, and what should we investigate?

How it works

We examine selected evidence across the seven TCI™ dimensions at a high level and speak with a small number of senior stakeholders. The scope stays deliberately narrow.

Best suited for

Transformations with a material concern that needs locating before a deeper assessment is commissioned.

What you receive

A concise Executive Confidence Diagnostic identifying the main confidence gaps, supporting evidence and areas for further investigation.

Decision supported

Whether a deeper review is justified and where it should focus. This is an entry paid engagement, not a reduced version of the full assessment.

AI Investment Confidence Review™Test whether an AI initiative or portfolio has the evidence to justify further funding or scale.

Does the evidence justify further AI investment or scale?

How it works

We apply the seven TCI™ dimensions through an AI-specific lens: strategic fit, adoption, technology and data readiness, operating economics, controls, risk and realised value. Targeted interviews test the investment thesis and scale assumptions.

Evidence examined

Relevant investment papers, use-case and benefit assumptions, adoption data, model and platform architecture, data controls, production performance, operating costs, risk registers and governance decisions. Material evidence is classified as documented, corroborated, asserted or unknown; evidence quality remains separate from the confidence judgement.

Best suited for

Organisations moving from experimentation to production, scaling GenAI, or reconsidering further investment in an AI initiative or portfolio.

What you receive

An AI Investment Confidence Report™ with an independent judgement, TCI™ confidence profile, evidence and value findings, material gaps, scale considerations and priority decisions.

Transformation Confidence Assessment™The full independent assessment of whether a major transformation is delivering funded value.

Are we creating the value we funded, and what does the evidence support next?

How it works

We test the original investment thesis against delivery, expenditure, adoption, sustainable capability and value realised. The review covers all seven TCI™ dimensions, classifies material evidence and uses executive interviews to test assumptions, ownership and contradictions.

Evidence examined

Relevant business cases, investment papers, financials, delivery measures, architecture, risks, benefits, governance and operating evidence. Material claims are checked against the available record rather than accepted from questionnaire responses.

Best suited for

Executives facing a significant investment, intervention or transformation decision across technology, data, cloud, ERP, digital, platform, AI or operating-model change.

What you receive

A Transformation Confidence Executive Report™, TCI™ Confidence Profile, independent qualitative judgements, evidence findings, value implications, material gaps, priority actions and decision considerations.
