Fictional scenario · Proposed report format

Five illustrative transformation scenarios.

These five fictional scenarios show how leaders might test evidence and discuss an investment decision. They are not client work or validated TCI assessments.

01

Cloud modernisation without operating model change

Migration milestones hit. But product teams don't feel faster and cloud costs are rising.

Read the scenario →
02

Enterprise data platform with low data confidence

Strong sponsorship, strategic importance. But business teams challenge data accuracy and ownership.

Read the scenario →
03

Agile transformation without leadership change

Teams trained, ceremonies running. But decision-making remained slow and funding stayed annual.

Read the scenario →
04

Platform engineering without internal customer adoption

Reusable capabilities built. But teams kept building their own tooling.

Read the scenario →
05

Transformation reporting green, value confidence red

Budget on track, milestones delivered, risks managed. But the Board asked: where is the value?

Read the scenario →

Cross-case insight. Each scenario shows a different gap between reported progress and evidence of lasting value. The evidence states are discussion prompts, not measured client results.
