How Akeel Advisory tests transformation confidence.

The proprietary Transformation Confidence Index™ (TCI™), used by Akeel Advisory, examines seven connected conditions that affect whether investment makes the organisation stronger and creates lasting value.

What TCI™ does

Progress records movement. Confidence requires evidence.

Transformation reporting often answers “Are we on plan?” TCI™ asks a harder question, “Why should we believe this transformation will create sustainable business value?”

The Index connects programme controls, benefits evidence and executive ownership. A score may inform judgement; it cannot make the investment decision for leaders.
