The framework

A clearer test of transformation confidence.

TCI™ examines whether leaders have enough connected evidence to trust the transformation outcome.

What TCI™ does

Move the conversation from status to confidence.

Transformation reporting often answers “Are we on plan?” TCI™ asks a harder question, “Why should we believe this transformation will create sustainable business value?”

The Index is a structured diagnostic lens. It does not replace programme controls, assurance or benefits tracking. It connects these sources into an executive view of confidence.

01

Strategic clarity

Tests whether the transformation has explicit outcomes, real choices, clear ownership and a shared definition of success.

02

Value realisation

Tests whether benefits have owners, baselines, credible measures and evidence of value already emerging.

03

Delivery confidence

Tests whether plans, dependencies, risks and decision cycles support a credible route to the outcome.

04

Adoption and capability

Tests whether behaviour, skills, incentives and the target operating model are changing with the technology.

05

Sustainability and governance

Tests whether accountability, controls, resilience and funding will hold after the programme phase ends.

TCI

Connected evidence

Confidence depends on coherence across all five dimensions, not a single strong score.

How to use the Index

A practical leadership diagnostic.

Use TCI™ at a portfolio review, strategy reset, investment decision or key transformation gateway.

Run the self-assessment

  1. Score the evidence

    Rate each question from weak or absent evidence to strong, repeatable evidence.

  2. Discuss the gaps

    Compare leadership views and locate the questions where scores differ.

  3. Test the narrative

    Check whether the transformation story reflects the evidence.

  4. Agree three actions

    Choose the evidence, decision or intervention needed next.
