What TCI does

Confidence requires evidence.

Progress records movement.

Transformation reporting often answers “Are we on plan?” TCI asks a harder question, “Why should we believe this transformation will create sustainable business value?”

The Index connects programme controls, benefits evidence and executive ownership. The confidence judgement is qualitative. It records what the evidence supports and where uncertainty remains.

    Seven connected dimensions

    Examine the conditions behind sustainable business value.

  2. 02 / TEST

    The strength of the evidence

    Distinguish records, supporting sources, claims and unknowns.

  3. 03 / INFORM

    The next decision

    Identify what the evidence supports and where uncertainty remains.

What the Index examines

Seven dimensions.
Connected evidence.

Confidence depends on connected evidence across all seven dimensions. A strong result in one area cannot resolve an evidence gap elsewhere.

  1. 01

    Strategic Alignment

    Is transformation clearly linked to enterprise strategy and measurable outcomes?

  2. 02

    Leadership Commitment

    Are leaders making decisions, removing constraints and accepting accountability for outcomes?

  3. 03

    Technology Readiness

    Can the architecture, data, controls and operating teams carry the technology after launch?

  4. 04

    People & Culture

    Are skills, behaviour, incentives and ways of working changing alongside the technology?

  5. 05

    Data & AI Confidence

    Can leaders trust the data used for decisions and AI? Are ownership, lineage and quality consistent?

  6. 06

    Delivery Excellence

    Can teams produce good outcomes predictably and at a sustainable pace?

  7. 07

    Business Value Realisation

    Is the delivered capability producing measurable business value with named owners?

Evidence before opinion

TCI separates what is known from what is believed.

Material evidence is classified as documented, corroborated, asserted or unknown. Evidence quality remains separate from the confidence judgement.

This allows executives to see not only what management believes, but how strongly the available evidence supports it.

  • Documented

    A record exists.

  • Corroborated

    Independent sources agree.

  • Asserted

    A claim awaits support.

  • Unknown

    The evidence is absent or unclear.

Using TCI

A practical leadership diagnostic.

Use TCI at a portfolio review, strategy reset, investment decision or key transformation gateway.

How to use the Index

  1. Examine the evidence

    Distinguish documented facts, management claims and material gaps.

  2. Discuss the gaps

    Compare leadership views and locate where judgements differ.

  3. Test the narrative

    Check whether the transformation story reflects the evidence.

  4. Agree three actions

    Choose the evidence, decision or intervention needed next.
