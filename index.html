The leadership problem

Progress reports often hide the confidence gap.

A programme might be green while confidence in the outcome stays low. Milestones, spend and activity matter. They do not prove sustainable business value.

The Transformation Confidence Index™ helps leaders examine the quality of evidence behind the transformation narrative. It turns broad confidence into a disciplined executive conversation.

01

Test the assumptions

Identify where confidence rests on intent, optimism or incomplete evidence.

02

Focus the dialogue

Give boards and executives a shared set of questions about value, risk and delivery.

03

Improve the response

Direct leadership attention towards the decisions and evidence which matter most.

Seven dimensions

A wider view of transformation performance.

TCI™ connects strategy, leadership, technology, people, data, delivery and value. Confidence grows when the evidence is coherent across all seven.

See the full framework

  1. Strategic Alignment

    Is transformation clearly linked to enterprise strategy?

  2. Leadership Commitment

    Are leaders making decisions and removing constraints?

  3. Technology Readiness

    Can the estate carry the technology after launch?

  4. People & Culture

    Are skills, behaviour and ways of working changing?

  5. Data Confidence

    Can leaders trust the data used for decisions?

  6. Delivery Excellence

    Can teams produce good outcomes predictably?

  7. Business Value Realisation

    Is capability producing measurable business value?
