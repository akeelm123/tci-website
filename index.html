How we test the evidence

The Transformation Confidence Index™ (TCI™) supports this sequence by examining seven dimensions: strategy, leadership, technology, people and culture, data and AI, delivery, and business value.

These are assessment dimensions, not stages. They help test whether the organisation can sustain the outcome and how much confidence the evidence supports.

We assess the evidence, revisit it at funding and benefits decisions, and advise on what management should do next.
