Illustrative scenario 1

Cloud modernisation without operating model change

A large enterprise invested heavily in cloud. Migration milestones hit. Infrastructure costs tracked. But product teams didn't feel faster.

This is a fictional teaching scenario, not a client engagement. Evidence gaps and timelines illustrate discussion only; they are not measured client results or a recommended client plan.

Executive concern

Relocating technology or transforming it?

Application teams still experienced slow provisioning. Security approvals remained manual. Cloud costs were increasing. The CIO wanted to know whether the programme was delivering transformation or simply moving workloads.

Persona lens

CIO Profile

Conditions to test

People & Culture and Business Value Realisation

Illustrative decision evidence

Evidence to weigh

Fictional example for discussion. These are questions to test, not measured TCI™ results.

What appears supported
Cloud migration milestones were met and infrastructure costs are being tracked.
What remains uncertain
Provisioning speed, manual approvals and cost ownership still limit the claimed operating benefit.
Evidence to test next
Compare developer lead time, approval cycle time, cloud unit costs and internal customer feedback with the original investment case.

Executive interpretation

The programme is technically progressing, but confidence is constrained.

Technology readiness is improving, but the organisation has not changed how teams consume, manage or optimise cloud capability. This creates a risk that cloud becomes a more expensive hosting model rather than a transformation enabler.

Example actions for discussion

30days

Stabilise

Clarify business outcomes expected from cloud adoption. Identify where delivery processes still slow teams down. Review cloud cost ownership. Establish internal customer feedback.

90days

Strengthen

Introduce platform product management. Define developer experience metrics. Improve automation across security, infrastructure and release. Align governance to speed, control and cost transparency.

180days

Sustain

Measure cloud value through time-to-market, resilience, cost optimisation and developer productivity. Embed FinOps. Shift reporting from migration volume to business capability created.

"Cloud transformation should not be measured by how many workloads move. It should be measured by whether the organisation can operate with greater speed, resilience, control and adaptability."
Akeel Munshi — Leadership lesson
