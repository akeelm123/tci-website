About this self-assessment

Best suited for

Leaders seeking an initial view before deciding whether independent investigation is warranted.

How it works

Download the TCI Self-Assessment: 21 questions across five themes. Rate the evidence, compare responses with your team and identify questions that need further investigation.

Evidence and engagement

The assessment is completed by the participant. There is no independent evidence review and no stakeholder interview process.

What makes it different

It provides a fast, accessible starting point for leaders who want to identify areas of strength, uncertainty or potential concern before deciding whether deeper investigation is required.

What you receive

A free PDF discussion guide for recording your views and agreeing where stronger evidence is needed.

What it does not do

This is not an independent assessment, audit, assurance opinion or evidence-based investment recommendation.

A discussion guide, not an independent assessment.

Your responses support reflection; they are not an independent evidence review or a validated confidence score.
